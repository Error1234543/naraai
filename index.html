About SONIC AI

A coding and research workspace.

SONIC AI is a personal coding and research assistant. It streams long answers, detects generated files, and lets you preview HTML, download single files, or download the whole project as a ZIP.

Conversations, settings and history live in localStorage on this device. Messages are sent through this site's own /api proxy to the model provider; the provider key never reaches the browser.
