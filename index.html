About SONIC AI

A focused, private chat front end.

SONIC AI is a lightweight assistant interface. It talks to any OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions endpoint, keeps your conversations in this browser only, and has no analytics, no tracking and no external dependencies.

Conversations, settings and history live in localStorage on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except the messages you send to the model endpoint configured in Settings.
